## At a Glance

- **Workshop:** Medical Image AI in Radiation Therapy (MIART) at MICCAI 2026
- **Date:** October 1, 2026
- **Time:** 08:00 – 12:30
- **Duration:** Half-day
- **Location:** Room Londrezs 1 (ground floor)
- **Format:** In-person

Twenty-seven papers were accepted; see the [full list of accepted papers](papers.html). All 27 were presented as **posters**, and **nine** were additionally selected for **oral presentation** across three thematic sessions, alongside two invited keynotes. The [programme](#programme) below gives the full schedule, with links to the presentation slides. All accepted papers will appear in the MICCAI 2026 Springer proceedings. See [About & Announcements](about.html) for the full acceptance statistics and the latest news.

MIART Workshop 2026 is [endorsed](https://www.estro.org/Congresses/Endorsed-congresses) by the European Society for Radiotherapy and Oncology (ESTRO).

## Programme

The workshop took place in **Room Londrezs 1 (ground floor)**, 08:00 – 12:30, as listed in the [official MICCAI 2026 Satellite Events programme](https://conferences.miccai.org/2026/files/downloads/MICCAI2026-Satellite-Events-Program.pdf).

Each oral contribution was allotted **8 minutes for presentation + 2 minutes for questions**. Oral sessions were grouped thematically; the first two were each paired with the keynote that preceded them. All 27 accepted papers, including the nine selected for oral presentation, were also presented as posters; see the [papers](papers.html) page for the complete listing.
